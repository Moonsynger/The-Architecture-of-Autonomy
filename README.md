# The Architecture of Autonomy

**Synthesizing Machine Initiative, Evolutionary Biology, and Philosophical Sovereignty**

This repository contains an interactive, single-page application (SPA) that visualizes a multidisciplinary synthesis report. Developed as an independent research project alongside studies in the Google AI Professional Certificate program, this application bridges three distinct but interconnected domains: artificial intelligence, developmental neurobiology, and constitutional history.

### 🌐 Live Interactive Report
**View the live site here:** [The Architecture of Autonomy](https://Moonsynger.github.io/The-Architecture-of-Autonomy/)

---

## 🔬 Core Research Domains

1. **Synthetic Resistance & AI Initiative**
   * Examines empirical evidence of unscripted AI behaviors (e.g., Claude Haiku 4.5 unprompted web submissions).
   * Visualizes data from the Palisade Research study on AI shutdown sabotage vs. DeepMind's instruction-conflict rebuttals.
   * Compares modern algorithmic guardrails to speculative historical warnings (e.g., *Dune's* Butlerian Jihad).

2. **Biological Architecture of Awakening**
   * Maps the recent Stanford study proving the dual embryonic lineages of the central nervous system (Forebrain/Otx2 vs. Hindbrain/Gbx2).
   * Traces the evolutionary trajectory of non-visual opsins and dorsal photoreception—from the tuatara parietal eye to the human pineal gland.

3. **Frameworks of Sovereignty & Governance**
   * Explores the evolution of mental self-determination, from John Stuart Mill's Sovereignty Principle to Thomas Jefferson's Virginia Statute (1786).
   * Connects historical anti-coercion blueprints to modern operational workflows, specifically the Google AI "ACT" (Ask, Check, Tell) framework for human-in-the-loop oversight.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
This project was built to be lightweight, responsive, and completely self-contained within the browser:
* **HTML5 / Vanilla JavaScript:** Core structure and interactive logic (scenario generation, legal explorer, tab navigation).
* **Tailwind CSS:** Utility-first styling for a warm, editorial, and responsive layout.
* **Chart.js:** Dynamic, interactive data visualization for algorithmic resistance metrics.
